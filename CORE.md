# The Immutable Core

These five principles cannot be changed by any decision of any crew, flotilla or the network as a whole. Everything else in [the manifesto](MANIFESTO.md) can change, under rules the members set themselves.

1. **Our course is beyond Earth.** The system exists for humanity's long journey into space, even if no one alive today will see its end.
2. **Every person belongs to themselves.** Everyone is free to join, to disagree and to leave. A person's name, history and data depend on no state, and not on the system either.
3. **No one is left alone in trouble.** A community whose members abandon each other will not survive into the next generation.
4. **Rules are made by those who live by them.** The system has no center that can give orders to crews.
5. **Deeds before promises.** Every promise must be backed before it is spoken aloud.

Corrections to spelling and wording that leave the meaning untouched are welcome. Changes to the meaning are not accepted.
